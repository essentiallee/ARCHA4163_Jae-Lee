NY Gallery Visualization

This project explores New York galleries through temporal and geographic visualization.

Digital Object: Gallery Timeline

The timeline organizes gallery shows by neighborhood and date. Move across the visualization to change the selected planning date, or click a show bar to locate its gallery on the map.

Geospatial Visualization: Mapbox

Select two galleries to generate a walking route between them. Click a selected gallery again to remove it.
